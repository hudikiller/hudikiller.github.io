CURE-Bench @ NeurIPS 2025

2025

CURE-Bench is a benchmark and competition for evaluating AI systems in clinically grounded drug decision-making and treatment planning, including treatment recommendation, drug safety and efficacy assessment, treatment design, and drug repurposing for diseases with limited therapeutic options.

In this project, I mainly worked on the construction of the knowledge base for the proposed AgentKB framework. Following the paper's Reason-Retrieve-Refine (3R) pipeline, I adapted the student model to parse and understand structured fields such as question, answer, prediction, and drug-infos, and then generated intermediate outputs using the prompt design described in the paper.

Based on the student-stage outputs, I further used the teacher model to integrate question, answer, and reasoning fields together with the paper's prompting strategy, producing structured knowledge entries containing agent-plan and agent-experience. These entries formed the final knowledge base used by the agent system for cross-task experience retrieval and plan refinement.

This work helped transform raw execution records into reusable task-solving knowledge, enabling the agent to better retrieve workflow-level templates and execution-level details for complex therapeutic reasoning tasks.

Intelligent Monitoring of Rapeseed Across the Full Growth Cycle Based on 3D Point Clouds

2025 - 2026

This project focused on 3D point-cloud-based phenotyping and structural analysis of rapeseed across the full growth cycle, aiming to replace low-efficiency manual measurements with an automated and fine-grained plant monitoring pipeline. The system targeted challenging stages such as dense foliage, flowering, and pod development, where occlusion and complex geometry make accurate analysis difficult.

Technically, the pipeline integrated 3D reconstruction, semantic segmentation, instance segmentation, and skeleton extraction. For organ-level parsing, the project adopted a PointNet++-based semantic segmentation approach to classify leaves, stems, flowers, and siliques by leveraging hierarchical local feature extraction under varying point densities. To separate individual organs of the same category, a PointGroup-style instance segmentation design with dual-branch feature extraction and dual-coordinate clustering was used to improve separation in dense and overlapping plant structures.

For higher-level structural understanding, the project explored a hybrid Point2Skeleton + Mamba framework for point-cloud skeletonization, combining geometric awareness with long-range sequence modeling. This design aimed to improve the accuracy, robustness, and efficiency of skeleton extraction, making it possible to better capture plant topology and growth patterns from raw 3D data.

Overall, this work emphasized the technical development of a full-stack 3D plant analysis framework for rapeseed, covering data acquisition, denoising and alignment, organ-aware segmentation, and topology-oriented structural modeling, with potential applications in phenotyping, crop monitoring, and yield-related trait analysis.

Fully Domestic Traditional Chinese Medicine Large Model

2025

This project aimed to build a fully domestic large model for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) to support the full pipeline of cultivation, research, and clinical diagnosis. The system addressed key challenges in TCM AI, including dependence on foreign compute infrastructure, heterogeneous multimodal data, fragmented knowledge systems, and hallucination risks in clinical decision support.

My main contributions focused on knowledge graph construction and the RAG/GraphRAG pipeline. Using DeepSeek-R1-32B, I participated in organizing and structuring a large-scale TCM knowledge base covering 140,000 data entries and more than 300 classical medical books. I worked on connecting heterogeneous sources such as ancient texts, clinical knowledge, and domain-specific medical resources into a retrieval-augmented framework that could better support reasoning and evidence grounding.

Technically, the project combined a domestic computing stack with knowledge graph construction, graph-enhanced retrieval, and explainable reasoning for TCM scenarios. The resulting system supported clinically relevant tasks such as syndrome reasoning, formula recommendation, and evidence-supported treatment assistance, and was demonstrated through a working demo system.

The project was recognized with Third Prize in the 2025 Huawei Ascend AI Innovation Competition (Southwest Regional) and National Third Prize in the 19th Challenge Cup National College Student Extracurricular Academic Science and Technology Competition (AI Special Track). It also advanced toward real-world deployment through collaboration with Shanghai Kunzhou Biotechnology Co., Ltd.
