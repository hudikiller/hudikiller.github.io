About Me

I am Di Hu, an undergraduate student in Data Science and Big Data Technology at Sichuan Agricultural University, Ya'an, China. My work spans large language models, multimodal intelligence, privacy-preserving learning, and AI for agriculture.

I have participated in multiple research projects and laboratory activities, with experience in benchmark construction, multimodal understanding, point cloud analysis, remote sensing segmentation, and domain-specific foundation models. I am especially interested in building practical and robust AI systems that connect model capability with real-world applications.
