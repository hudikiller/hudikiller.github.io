About Me

I am Di Hu, a B.Eng. candidate in Data Science and Big Data Technology at Sichuan Agricultural University (SICAU, Project 211), with a GPA of 4.02/5.00. My work focuses on large language models, agent systems, multimodal AI, and applied intelligence for healthcare and agriculture.

MAS: Automatic Error Attribution in Multi-Agent Systems

Core Algorithm Contributor

May 2025 - Dec 2025
  • Framed MAS execution as a replayable directed cyclic trajectory to support full-chain tracing over complex reasoning logs.
  • Integrated all-at-once, step-by-step, and binary-search attribution strategies to locate the earliest destabilization point in coding and general reasoning tasks.
  • Built the MAST annotation standard with 3 classes and 14 failure patterns, a grounded-theory rule base, standardized attribution reports, and Cohen's kappa expert-consistency evaluation.
  • Developed failure heatmaps, causal-chain reports, and intervention templates such as role termination and high-level verification to accelerate diagnosis and repair.

Multi-Agent Systems Fault Attribution Causal Analysis Benchmarking

Yimai Xiangcheng: Fully Domestic Traditional Chinese Medicine Foundation Model

Project Lead

Jul 2024 - Jul 2025
  • Led a national-key-lab-backed effort to build a fully domestic TCM foundation-model stack under compute and data heterogeneity constraints.
  • Proposed the TCM-MMKG fusion pipeline with BiLSTM-CRF and TransE, structuring information from 397 classical texts and 148,000 papers into a 1.2M-node knowledge graph.
  • Developed a GraphRAG-based neural-symbolic reasoning engine with FAISS retrieval and TCM rule bases, including mappings between TCM concepts and Western indicators such as serum creatinine.
  • Optimized training on an Ascend 910B cluster, reducing per-case processing time from 23 minutes to 4.6 minutes and inference latency to 2-3 seconds.
  • Won a National Third Prize in the 19th Challenge Cup and earned three Huawei Ascend technical certifications.
